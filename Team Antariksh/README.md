# Team Antariksh

## Overview

### BPTD (Tender Descender) Mechanism
The **Tender Descender** is a specialized parachute release device crucial for the dual-deployment recovery systems of high-power sounding rockets. It acts as a mechanical linkage that keeps the main parachute tightly reefed, preventing premature inflation upon the initial apogee ejection. Once the rocket descends to a predetermined safe altitude, an electronic match triggers a small internal charge, splitting the descender into two parts. This cleanly severs the tether and allows the main parachute to fully deploy for a controlled touchdown. The BPTD test video below demonstrates the successful ground validation of this actuation mechanism.

### Body Tube Separation
The **Body Tube Separation** mechanism utilizes a controlled black powder charge to reliably separate the rocket's structural body tubes during the recovery sequence. In this design, the black powder is housed within custom 3D-printed canisters. A specialized cap is secured using industrial-grade cyanoacrylate (superglue) to maintain a strict hermetic seal and allow pressure to build rapidly. Upon ignition, the immense internal pressure aggressively bursts the cap, providing the precise kinetic force required to cleanly separate the body tubes without damaging internal components.
